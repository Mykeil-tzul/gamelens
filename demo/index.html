    How the search is evaluated

    ReplayGPT retrieves text annotations using basketball synonyms, matching-word coverage, and weighted tags. Optional meaning search uses MiniLM embeddings in your browser to find related wording. Similarity is a ranking signal and does not establish that a play happened. It shows up to three supported matches and reports no match when evidence is missing. The diagnostic query set checks clip retrieval and unsupported questions. It measures this small index, with no claim of general video understanding.

    What worked, what missed

    On three manually labeled frames from the outdoor pickup benchmark, GameLens matched 15 of 16 on-court player boxes at 50% overlap. There were 17 extra detections. Precision was 47% and recall was 94% on this small sample. Spectators and other people can be picked up by the detector. That evaluation measured detection boxes.
