What worked, what missed

On three manually labeled frames from the outdoor pickup benchmark, GameLens matched 15 of 16 on-court player boxes at 50% overlap. There were 17 extra detections. Precision was 47% and recall was 94% on this small sample. Spectators and other people can be picked up by the detector. Identity accuracy was not measured.
